[[brandtFacilitatingCollaborationDesign2004a]]
Design Games construct scenarios that are both concrete and flexible. They represent a particular interpretation of a design situation. They are deliberately incomplete to be open for negotiation and change. 

[[brandtDesigningExploratoryDesign2006]]
Scenarios can be constructed to restructure the current situation and provide new insights. Those can be enacted with [[Acting]] using simple [[Game pieces]]. 

Scenario construction can be used for creating and testing design ideas. Generic [[Game pieces]] props may be provided or the users encouraged to produce their own. Having users create and explore [[Acting]] scenarios in their own environment puts them on more equal footing to the design team [[Power relations]].

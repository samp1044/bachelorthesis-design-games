Use of stylized [[Game pieces]] prompts participants to be more explicit about their understandings and views when playing. 

[[vaajakallioDesignGamesCodesign2014]]
In contrast to a typical game in design games [[Rules]] are often open for reinterpretation [p.6] [[Open ended]]. Vague rules and [[Game pieces]] trigger players to be explicit in describing how they understand and interpret them. This supports building a [[Common language]] among participants. [p.6]. 

**Predesigned material** is of great help in setting a frame and starting points for the design collaboration [p.12] [[Input]] [[Context]]. **Open-ended and ambiguous tasks** help participants to propose new interpretations and alternative solutions. [p.12].

[[Combining elements that do not fit together]]
From the surrealist movement. Some creativity techniques are based on combining elements which normally do not fit together. [[brandtDesigningExploratoryDesign2006]]

[[Rules]]
Rules set the boundaries for whats possible and also structure the play of the game. Shift from the goals of the design process to the rules of a game makes it easier to generate design moves. Further, restrictions on idea generation can improve the outcome. [[brandtFacilitatingCollaborationDesign2004a]]

[[Scenarios]]
Design Games construct scenarios that are both concrete and flexible. They represent a particular interpretation of a design situation. They are deliberately incomplete to be open for negotiation and change [[brandtFacilitatingCollaborationDesign2004a]]

Scenario construction can be used for creating and testing design ideas. Generic Game pieces props may be provided or the users encouraged to produce their own [[brandtDesigningExploratoryDesign2006]]

[[Split in groups]]
Participants play the same game in parallel, split in groups. Thus each group comes to their own conclusion [[eriksenTakingDesignGames2014]]

Participants were **divided into groups**. An additional Facilitator led the workshop and presented the agenda, cards and rules. Participants at **first individually created ideas** to then share them with the rest of their team members [[bunaesRulesPlayBalance2024]]

Mentions of splitting in groups also in the papers: [[gudiksenExplicatingStakeholderCriteria2017]], [[kjaersgaardDesignGamesFieldwork2021]], [[nymarkernstsenTechnologyCardsDesign2023]]

[[Tangible Game Material]]
Like [[Game board]], [[cards]]. They abstract real-life objects but are not too concrete to be open to interpretation. E.g. Game boards with concentric circles when designing an office layout. Can be interpreted as space centering around the individual worker vs. multiple circles can be interpreted as groups working together [[brandtFacilitatingCollaborationDesign2004a]]

**Visual and tangible components** of the design games are **generative tools**. They aim at creating a platform for shared focus of attention. They also provide a documentation and reminder during and after the co-design gathering [[vaajakallioDesignGamesCodesign2014]]

Ease of moving around the cards on the table (**as tangible pieces**) and pairing them enabled creation of links across themes and gain new perspectives into underlying causes of some challenges faced [[hatamiFacilitatingServiceInteractions2016]]

The **game board** seemed to help and limit the solutions participants came up with. [p.6] Results of previous stages were always visible to the participants [[gudiksenExplicatingStakeholderCriteria2017]]

**Externalized representations** are important during design development. One approach is to organize blocks of content into **tangible [[cards]]**. Simply **shuffling through the cards** and posing the questions creates an opportunity to remember to address more issues than might be in the initial project brief [[royCardbasedDesignTools2019]]

Geistesblitz bildet sich im Zusammenspiel mit den materiellen Artefakten des Spiels aus: Spielfeldern, Linien, Sitzmöglichkeiten, die anderen Teilnehmenden [[fikarGroundedTheoryZur2023]]

physical gestures** involved in **holding, moving and grouping cards** can aid cognition and help simplify the complexity of a design problem [[nymarkernstsenTechnologyCardsDesign2023]]

[[Win]]
Rarely **competition** in design games. But **tension** (entailing elements of chance and a desire to succeed) is a quality of design games. [[vaajakallioDesignGamesCodesign2014]]

Example game Pollutaplop: Focuses players around **two [[Goal]]s - one individual, one cooperative** [p.4]. Misalignment between individual and cooperative goal leads to a broken game here. Players fight the game. They concentrate on their individual goal, which hurts the cooperative goal [[kessnerWickedPlayWicked2020]]

A **winning condition and [[rules]]** to guide argumentation makes it **easier to reach consensus** in the design game, which is described as more troublesome in classic co-design workshops with strangers [[zhangAddressingCulturalInertias2024]]

[[Rules]]
Playing by the Rules plays down power relations between participants. [[brandtFacilitatingCollaborationDesign2004a]], [[brandtDesigningExploratoryDesign2006]]

Spieler fügen sich in **verregelte Rollen** ein, die ihnen bestimmte Spielräume einräumen. Regeln geben vor welchen Zweck die Spieler verfolgen, was Kriterien für Erfolg sind und welche Mittel zur Zielerreichung legitim sind. Regel stellen also den Freiraum durch Ausschluss des Alltags her und bilden gleichzeitig auch seine Grenzen [[fikarGroundedTheoryZur2023]]

**Rules** of the design game **can quickly change the communication patterns** of participants. They **prompt players to voluntarily overcome unnecessary obstacles**, for example by prescribing **when and where players should explicitly discuss a certain theme** with stakeholders. This **creates a shift from implicit communication to more explicit communication** patterns [[zhangAddressingCulturalInertias2024]]

[[Turn taking]]
Turn taking allows participation on more equal terms, leveling Power relations. [[brandtDesigningExploratoryDesign2006]]

Turn taking, symbolized through a figure. Often used with the aim to equalize Power relations. It divides attention among human players. [[eriksenTakingDesignGames2014]]

Turn taking can have a levelling effect for participants, giving each player equal opportunity to take part in playing the game. This also breaks existing hierarchies, giving participants an equal footing among themselves [[hatamiFacilitatingServiceInteractions2016]]

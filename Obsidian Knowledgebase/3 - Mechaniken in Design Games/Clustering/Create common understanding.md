[[Rules]]
Tangible Game Material and Rules help bridge the gap between different languages of the participants. They create a common ground / Common language everyone can relate to. [[brandtDesigningExploratoryDesign2006]]

Rules** convey what players can or cannot do in the temporary world. In contrast to a typical game in design games rules are often open for reinterpretation. Vague rules and Game pieces trigger players to be explicit in describing how they understand and interpret them. This supports building a Common language among participants. [[vaajakallioDesignGamesCodesign2014]]

**Rules** of the design game **can quickly change the communication patterns** of participants. They **prompt players to voluntarily overcome unnecessary obstacles**, for example by prescribing **when and where players should explicitly discuss a certain theme** with stakeholders. This **creates a shift from implicit communication to more explicit communication** patterns [[zhangAddressingCulturalInertias2024]]

[[Tangible Game Material]]
Being tangible helps all participants to keep track of their functions and role within the negotiation. [[brandtFacilitatingCollaborationDesign2004a]]

They make arguments tangible and help participants to focus. They create a common ground everybody can relate to and act as things-to-think-with. They allow different participants to read and interpret the material differently. They are rich enough in content to span the gap between different understandings [[brandtFacilitatingCollaborationDesign2004a]]

User Game has Moment cards and Sign-cards to provide Input for a conceptual framework for stories created by the participants. Game state and Game pieces are placed centrally so every participant can see the current state of information. 
Game materials help bridge the gap between different languages of the participants. [[brandtDesigningExploratoryDesign2006]]

Explanation of the game by Facilitator by moving and referring to the game materials.
Game material such as a Game board so everyone can see the current state. Preselected and preprinted Controversity Gameboard Game pieces - set a topic (Input)for dialogue and negotiations. Also preprinted proposals so participants can point towards details during discussions. 
Personal tokens to argue/bet with. By moving the proposal tokens the discussion is captured physically on the board for everyone to see. 
In each Turn taking the current turn taker reads out the question on the controversity Game board. It thus becomes the center of attention and sets the scene. [[eriksenTakingDesignGames2014]]

Cards are placed on the table to provide each participants with a complete picture [[hatamiFacilitatingServiceInteractions2016]]

Use of a **game board** that illustrated the process (a small hill, barriers illustrating challenges) also a ball that illustrated the process.

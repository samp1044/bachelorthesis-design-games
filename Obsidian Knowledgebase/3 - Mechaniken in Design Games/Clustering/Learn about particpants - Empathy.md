[[Acting]] 
Acting out [[Scenarios]] from a users view helps to create [[empathy]] for the users and situations of use.  [[brandtFacilitatingCollaborationDesign2004a]]

**Enacted performance**, like stage performers, helps in gaining empathy of experiences of the persons to design for [p.7]. Design games also allow participants to **switch roles** and gain new perceptions on a topic and empathic understanding of other peoples experiences. [[vaajakallioDesignGamesCodesign2014]]

[[Boundary object]]
They allow players to find out how other interpret the material. Those objects carry shared meaning. They make arguments tangible. [[brandtFacilitatingCollaborationDesign2004a]]

(Rules as Boundary object): **Teilnehmer** gelten als **Träger kultureller Wahrnehmungs, Bewertungs und Handlungsschemata**, **Regel** werden also **nicht völlig frei interpretiert**, sondern vor dem Hintergrund der Erfahrungen und Erwartungen der Spielenden [[fikarGroundedTheoryZur2023]]

(Rules as Boundary object): Acting as **boundary objects** between stakeholders in the design process, design games can set rules for collaborative activities and bring in new perspectives (Kwiatkowska 2014) [[nymarkernstsenTechnologyCardsDesign2023]] 

[[Chance]]
Dads-round: Turning table as **game board** that allows participants to pick a random theme. Also a story **card can be selected by chance** or **supplied by the game master**. Additionally a Dice is present with predefined roles (mother, sibling, grandparents, ..) through their perspective the story should be discussed. This allows to empathize with this role.

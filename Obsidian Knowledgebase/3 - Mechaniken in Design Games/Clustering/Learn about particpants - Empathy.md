[[Acting]] 
Acting out [[Scenarios]] from a users view helps to create [[empathy]] for the users and situations of use.  [[brandtFacilitatingCollaborationDesign2004a]]

**Enacted performance**, like stage performers, helps in gaining empathy of experiences of the persons to design for [p.7]. Design games also allow participants to **switch roles** and gain new perceptions on a topic and empathic understanding of other peoples experiences. [[vaajakallioDesignGamesCodesign2014]]

By **enacting** the scenarios the different approaches, agendas, choices of scenes of the participants get visible. [[kjaersgaardDesignGamesFieldwork2021]]

[[Boundary object]]
They allow players to find out how other interpret the material. Those objects carry shared meaning. They make arguments tangible. [[brandtFacilitatingCollaborationDesign2004a]]

(Rules as Boundary object): **Teilnehmer** gelten als **Träger kultureller Wahrnehmungs, Bewertungs und Handlungsschemata**, **Regel** werden also **nicht völlig frei interpretiert**, sondern vor dem Hintergrund der Erfahrungen und Erwartungen der Spielenden [[fikarGroundedTheoryZur2023]]

(Rules as Boundary object): Acting as **boundary objects** between stakeholders in the design process, design games can set rules for collaborative activities and bring in new perspectives (Kwiatkowska 2014) [[nymarkernstsenTechnologyCardsDesign2023]] 

[[Chance]]
Dads-round: Turning table as **game board** that allows participants to pick a random theme. Also a story **card can be selected by chance** or **supplied by the game master**. Additionally a Dice is present with predefined roles (mother, sibling, grandparents, ..) through their perspective the story should be discussed. This allows to empathize with this role. [[kjaersgaardDesignGamesFieldwork2021]]

[[Magic if]]
When Acting Scenarios actress has to ask herself questions like "if the character was in this situation - how would she react"? [[brandtDesigningExploratoryDesign2006]]

[[Participant concepts]]
Story-card game: Response of consultants to the stories thus created were suprisingly unfamiliar and different from those of the design researchers. The game does not simply generate data or facilitate collaboration but also **serves as a tool for exploring and challenging perspective** of both researchers and participants [[kjaersgaardDesignGamesFieldwork2021]] 

Design Games sind abgeschlossene Interaktionssysteme. Das erzeugt ein Spannungsverhältnis zwischen offener Interpretation und den erwarteten Ergebnissen des Design Games [p.9]. Design Games schlagen Brücken zum Alltag der Teilnehmenden um Schlüsse über diesen zu ermöglichen. [[fikarGroundedTheoryZur2023]]

[[Roles]]
**Role-play** activity. In performances, play and games the reactions of performers and audience are real, even though the actions that trigger these are fictional [[vaajakallioDesignGamesCodesign2014]] 

Spieler fügen sich in **verregelte Rollen** ein, die ihnen bestimmte Spielräume einräumen
**Perspektivenwechsel** erfolgt für die Spielenden über das Gestalten von Aktionen als Reaktion auf die Aktionen anderer Spielende [[fikarGroundedTheoryZur2023]]

[[Rules]]
Since **Rules** are easily associated with board and card games, they work as a bridge between ordinary work practices and the special play sphere [[vaajakallioDesignGamesCodesign2014]]

Interaktives Interpretieren der Regeln berichtet über die Spielenden und ihre Praxisfelder. **Teilnehmer** gelten als **Träger kultureller Wahrnehmungs, Bewertungs und Handlungsschemata**, **Regel** werden also **nicht völlig frei interpretiert**, sondern vor dem Hintergrund der Erfahrungen und Erwartungen der Spielenden [[fikarGroundedTheoryZur2023]]

[[Tangible Game Material]]
They also function as Boundary object between people with various competencies. They allow players to also find out how other interpret the material [[brandtFacilitatingCollaborationDesign2004a]]

Using tangible pieces allow participants to use visual, auditory and tactile senses, which evoke more reflections and comments than otherwise. [[brandtDesigningExploratoryDesign2006]]

Gamepieces and Gameboard allowed participants to discuss concepts through the eyes of different stakeholders [[gudiksenExplicatingStakeholderCriteria2017]]

Participants enacted scenarios using **tangible** game pieces. They used **abstracted objects** like a writeable board, figures, wood blocks, plasticine to represent scenarios they experienced [[kjaersgaardDesignGamesFieldwork2021]]

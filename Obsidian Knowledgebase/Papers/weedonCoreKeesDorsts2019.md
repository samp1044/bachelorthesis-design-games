# The Core of Kees Dorst’s Design Thinking: A Literature Review (2019)

Considers boundary between ill-structured and well-structured problems as presented by Simon (1973) as fuzzy. 

The very identity of a problem can not be completely defined at the outset of the problem-solving process. Instead the design problem and its solution "coevolve" throughout the process of design work as designers seek to articulate "both the formulation of a problem and ideas for a solution, with constant iteration of analysis, synthesis, and evaluation processes between the **two notational design 'spaces'** - **problem space** and **solution space**"

Suggested the term "design problem" might be inaccurate. Might instead be more accurate to consider design work as resolving **paradoxes**. Paradox as in: a complex statement that consists of two or more conflicting statements. All the statements that make up the paradox are true or valid, but they cannot be combined. 

Approach to solve them is to frame the paradox so the conflicts can be approached from new and illuminating perspectives. 

**Frame Creation Model**. 

**Abductive reasoning**: In contrast to deductive and inductive reasoning. Starts with a "value" to create but does not settle on a means to achieve it. To obtain a certain value, designers need to linguistically frame the situation in such a way that conflicting discourses can be resolved. 

Resolution begins when designers analyze the broader context of the problem through a phenomenological search for **"themes"**. E.g. Drunk people in Sydney district. _Key themes_ that emerged: people concerned where overwhelmingly young (non-criminals) wanting to have a good time (= the _value_ to be achieved). _Reframing_ the problem provided different tactics and ways to think about the problem. 

Process of framing moves design thinking from a way of inventing solutions to a way of thinking differently about wicked problems. 

Conceptualizing power in mapping one domain of relations to another (e.g. bar patrons and their environment mapped to a music festival).

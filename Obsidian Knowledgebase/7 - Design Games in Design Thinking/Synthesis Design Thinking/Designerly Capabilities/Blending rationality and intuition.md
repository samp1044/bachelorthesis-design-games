[[schonReflectivePractitionerHow2009]]
**Tacit knowledge**: Intuitive behaviours, often internalized when learning something, that happen automatically. We often do not think about it and cannot tell when asked based on what criteria we decided something. 
**Reflection-in-action**: Often when something surprising happened. Breaks out of the tacit knowledge and lets Practitioners reflect on the criteria used to decide something, how they analyzed something, how they came to this conclusion. Can happen during the action itself. Can lead to new theories. 

[[micheliDoingDesignThinking2019]]
Through a systematic literature review and following card sorting exercise 10 attributes of _design thinking_ have been identified [p.7] 
- **Blending rationality and intuition**: Rather than replacing analytical logic, _design thinking_ dynamically blends rational, evaluative research with intuitive insights to drive innovation.

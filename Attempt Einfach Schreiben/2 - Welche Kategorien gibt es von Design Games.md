[Designing exploratory design games: a framework for participation in Participatory Design? - Brandt - 2006]
Focuses on exploratory Design Games [p.2]. Categorizes them along aspects of designing [p.3] into 4 kinds of exploratory design games [p.5] The Games Design depends on the intention and the people to be involved [p.5] :

_Games to Conceptualize Designing_ [p.3]
Games utilize game pieces on a game board. Those pieces are stylized and abstracted to eliminate functional knowledge and experiences designers already bring with them. Purpose of the games is to be a tool for research. 

_Exchange Perspective Games_ [p.3-4]
Uses the element of chance. Often starts with pieces set in the real world and then builds on change and imagination to combine them in new ways. 

_Negotiation and Work-flow Oriented Games_ [p.4]
Focus on creating a common understanding of the work context to design for. Participants usually play themselves. Objective is also to create a common language. 

_Scenario Oriented Games_ [p.4-5]
Participants enact scenarios using simple props. Focus is often on the functionality of the artifact to be designed. 

Further categorized in aspects that distinquish powerful exploratory design games from less powerful ones [p.5 onward] :

_Staging Explorative As-if-worlds_ [p.7-8]
Aim at creating future visions. Games have explicit rules for participation. Assignments are open ended, giving participants the possibility to influence what to focus on in the design. Played in informal atmosphere [p.8]

_Enacting Exploratory Design Games_ [p.8-9]
Uses scenarios that are enacted to develop empathy for the users and situations of use; and to design while acting out scenarios in situ [p.8].
